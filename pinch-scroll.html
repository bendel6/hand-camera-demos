SEC 01 — INTRO

Your hand is the scroll wheel.

This page is driven by a single gesture: bring your thumb and index finger together to engage, then drift your hand up or down. The camera-derived skeleton runs through MediaPipe Hands in your browser; no frames leave the device.

The preview in the bottom right shows what the model sees. Joints are tracked in 21 landmarks per hand; we only care about two of them — landmark 4 (thumb tip) and landmark 8 (index tip).

SEC 02 — PINCH

Hysteresis avoids flicker.

Engagement and release use different thresholds. You enter pinch mode when the normalized thumb–index distance drops below PINCH_THRESHOLD, and you stay pinched until it rises above the larger RELEASE_THRESHOLD.

This is the same trick a Schmitt trigger uses to ignore noise around an edge. Without it, a half-pinched hand at the boundary toggles dozens of times per second and the page jitters.

SEC 03 — MOTION

Anchor, then track delta.

On pinch start we capture the current midpoint Y of the two fingertips and the current window.scrollY. From that frame on, the scroll position is set to anchorScroll - (currentY - anchorY) * GAIN.

Moving your hand up makes the page scroll down — the same direction your finger pushes content on a touchscreen. Release the pinch to drop the anchor; the next pinch starts a fresh interaction.

SEC 04 — SMOOTHING

Raw landmarks shimmer.

Per-frame model output is noisy at the millimeter scale. An exponential moving average on the midpoint with factor SMOOTHING ≈ 0.55 trades a few frames of latency for a much calmer cursor.

A small DEAD_ZONE on the delta keeps tiny hand tremors from creeping the page when you mean to hold still.

SEC 05 — LOSS

If the hand vanishes.

Walking out of frame mid-pinch shouldn't strand you in a phantom drag. After three consecutive frames with no detected hand, the pinch is cancelled. Bringing your hand back in starts a new interaction with a fresh anchor.

SEC 06 — TWEAK

All knobs are at the top.

Open pinch-scroll.html and find the consts: PINCH_THRESHOLD, RELEASE_THRESHOLD, GAIN, DEAD_ZONE, SMOOTHING. Refresh — no build step.
